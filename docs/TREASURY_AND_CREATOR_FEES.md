# Treasury and creator fee status

The canonical current-state description is [WELP swap fees and treasury status](FEES_AND_TREASURY.md).

In brief: Jupiter referral collection is a separate optional fee rail. Its referral proceeds are claimable by the configured partner wallet and do not automatically enter WELP's treasury. The checked-in 100-bps protocol, 50-bps creator, 2-bps genesis, and 14-day 60/40 calculations are policy math only. Mainnet does not currently have an executable WELP treasury program at the configured launch/treasury address, and no epoch payout has occurred.
